Privacy policy

Kingdom Welfare app · Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

The Kingdom Welfare app is published for Kingdom Welfare (KWR), a church in Harare and Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe. The app was developed by Tapiwa Sande (tapiwasande.com), who is the developer and the contact for this policy.

What the app stores on your phone

Your highlights, bookmarks, notes, chosen Bible translation, text size and last reading place are saved only on your phone. The developer and the church cannot see them, and they are not uploaded anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted.

What we collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location or contacts, and we do not use analytics or trackers of our own.

Internet use

The Bible works fully offline. The app uses the internet to show the latest videos from the church's YouTube channels and to play them, to load the church's announcements, events, articles and audio sermons (stored on Google Firebase), and to load its fonts. When you do this, your device contacts Google, Firebase and YouTube, which may receive your IP address and device information. Their handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. The app only reads this content: it does not send anything about you to Firebase.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other apps or websites, such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, your phone dialer and Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Sharing

When you share a verse or copy giving details, the text goes only to the app you choose. The giving screen shows bank and EcoCash details for you to copy. The app does not process payments.

Children

The app is a general church app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to the developer, Tapiwa Sande, through tapiwasande.com.
